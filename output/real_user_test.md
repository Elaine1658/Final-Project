# Real-user test record

This record summarizes one real participant test. No identifying information is included. The participant's feedback was consistent with the walkthrough below.

## Participant and scenario

- Participant: real user; identifying details intentionally omitted
- Scenario: Yale Family Weekend, rainy weather, 350 expected attendees
- Task: use the control tower to create a draft prep plan and explain what the manager should do next

## Observed walkthrough

1. The participant identified the product purpose from the title and introductory text.
2. The participant used the What-if scenario controls for rainy weather and 350 expected attendees.
3. The participant ran the scenario and reviewed the predicted demand and recommended prep numbers.
4. The participant noticed that soup and sandwich had both a shortage risk and a one-day expiry warning.
5. The participant used the Manager snapshot to identify the highest-priority item.
6. The participant reviewed Agent activity and saw the sales, inventory, and context tool calls.
7. The participant understood that the result was a draft and that a manager needed to approve it.
8. The participant approved the draft and saw the confirmation that no external order was placed.

## Feedback

- The dashboard made the main action easy to find.
- The difference between predicted demand and recommended prep was understandable.
- The shortage and expiry warnings made the recommendation feel more trustworthy because the system explained what needed attention.
- The What-if controls were useful for exploring different attendance and weather assumptions.
- The participant suggested adding historical user ingredient preferences to help improve future dining decisions.

## Product implication of the preference suggestion

A future version could use opt-in, aggregated preference signals to identify meals or ingredients that users are more likely to choose. Preference data should not expose an individual's eating history or override allergy, expiry, inventory, or food-safety rules.

## Evidence boundary

This is a qualitative test with one participant. It supports usability observations and the product suggestion above. It does not claim statistically representative feedback, measured time savings, improved forecast accuracy, or production waste reduction.
