# Real-user test worksheet

Use this worksheet during a 10-15 minute test. Do not record private information. Ask the participant for permission before taking a screenshot or recording the screen.

- Date:
- Role or relevant experience: e.g. student event organizer, dining worker, restaurant worker
- Name: do not include unless the participant explicitly agrees
- Consent to anonymous notes: Yes / No
- Consent to screenshot or screen recording: Yes / No

## Task script

Say:

> Imagine you are helping plan tomorrow's campus dining service. There may be a large event, the weather may change, and the kitchen has limited inventory. Please use this app to create a plan and tell me what you would do next.

Do not explain every button before the participant tries it. Observe what they do first.

Suggested scenario:

- Weather: Rain
- Expected attendance: 350
- Event: Yale Family Weekend

## Ask after the task

1. What would you expect this tool to help you with?
2. Which part of the screen was most useful?
3. Was any number or warning unclear?
4. Would you trust this as a draft for tomorrow's planning? Why or why not?
5. What would you change before using it in real work?
